Beijing · The APEC Summit

Trump Lands in China for First-Ever Three-Way Summit

By the Ledger's Wire Staff · Beijing, Wednesday

President Donald Trump touched down in China on Wednesday ahead of Thursday's unprecedented three-way summit with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin — the first time the leaders of the United States, China and Russia have ever sat at one table. Xi and Putin opened the day by jointly blasting Western "protectionism" and sanctions. The Iran war, nuclear arms and trade top an agenda heavy with consequence, and the whole world is watching Beijing.

The Gulf · War

US Strikes Iran's Qeshm Island

American forces struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, answering overnight Iranian fire on US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain — the first attack on Kuwait since September. Bahrain says it has now intercepted Iranian attacks three times in a week.

Space · NASA

Voyager 1 Reaches One Light-Day

NASA's Voyager 1 on Wednesday became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth — 16.1 billion miles out, 49 years after launch. Its radio signals now take a full 24 hours to come home.

Washington · Politics

Jeffries Nominated for Speaker

House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker, 251 to 11. If the full House confirms him in January, the Brooklyn congressman would become the first Black Speaker in American history.

Business · Chips

Nvidia Beats Forecasts; Huang Era Ends

Nvidia's earnings beat expectations on Wednesday, but the chip giant's board shut the door on a return for ousted chief executive Jensen Huang, saying he is gone for good — and ending months of speculation on Wall Street.
